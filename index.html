About Me

Anā mufakkirun wa mukhaṭṭiṭun aghmuru fī ‘ālam al-falsafah. Tatamḥaru mī’ūlī fī istikshāfi al-afkāri al-jadīdah wa al-manāhiji al-ibdā‘iyyah fī ḥalli al-masā'ikil.

Ma‘a khalfīyah ta‘līmīyah tarkizu ‘alā al-falsafah, anā mū’minun bi’anna al-fikr al-naqdīyy huwa al-miftāḥu limuwājahat taḥaddiyāti al-mustaqbali.
